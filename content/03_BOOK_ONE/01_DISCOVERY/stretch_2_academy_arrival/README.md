## What changed

- **Ch. 8 was rebuilt** and absorbed the arrival material orphaned in Ch. 9, so
  the arrival now precedes the quartet and the Syndicate threat arrives on day
  one instead of on day two.
- **Ch. 9 no longer presents Harlow as a guest speaker.** He is Lyra's teacher
  lecturing in his own subject in his own building.
- The epigraph is attributed to Weaver, removing a fourth academic "Dr. Wei".
- `Mr. Alvarez` → **Mr. Reyes**; Milo has one surname in the book.
- The pendant is the **jade** everywhere. It is not the Animus Argenti, and
  Harlow's is the **origami crane** everywhere.
- The board in Ch. 8 defines severity, node-failure projection, local distortion
  and collector capacity as four different questions, so that the numbers later
  in the book are auditable.

## Exit test

A reader can say: what the letter proved, who wrote it, who the Security Chief
is, why the counter has a date on it, and what Lyra is now concealing and from
whom.
