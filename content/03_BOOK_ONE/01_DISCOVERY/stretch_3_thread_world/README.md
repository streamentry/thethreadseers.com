## What changed

- **The aunt is on the page as a named person with a job**: Dr. Mei-Hua Chen,
  Ethicist and Keeper of Records, Lyra's aunt, and the person who can release or
  withhold a record. She is not the Headmistress. Weaver is.
- Ch. 12A is `Elder Adebayo Adeyemi`, *he/him*, throughout the book.
- Ch. 12A's announcement is of **Dr. Mei-Hua Chen's** absence, and the man in
  the corridor afterwards is Lyra's father. Two Chens, two jobs, no ambiguity.
- `Elara Vance` throughout. The two sisters are the **Vances**; the Security
  Chief is a Blackwood and is not related to anybody here.
- Ch. 14 ends with the Animus Argenti chamber destroyed and the blueprint's twin
  — the machine itself — buried, which is why the pendant matters afterwards
  and why there is a Conduit *under the East Tower* rather than in the Maze.

## Exit test

A reader can name the machine, say what it does to a body, say who funded it,
and explain why the Academy's public account of the fourteenth is a lie that
Lyra is now inside of.
