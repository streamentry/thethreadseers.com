## What changed

- **The clocks are medical.** The offer is finite and real and refuses itself on
  arithmetic rather than on heroism.
- **The projection is a projection**, made on a console, with a modelled voice,
  dated 2009, showing a pregnant Lin. Lyra writes the discrepancy down in the
  dark and does not act on it in the dark.
- **The liberation is a question, asked once.** Thirty-seven are released; two
  are not, because one of them cannot be moved without dying and one cannot hear
  the question. The draft's restocking of the machine is gone.
- **31/32 no longer stage the same scene twice.** Ch. 32 opens in an anteroom
  at **15:10 on 2 October**, the same day as Ch. 31 and later in the same
  sitting, and does not restage the floor. The three options are read out from
  a written memorandum (`32:43`, the section headed THE TERMS), a decision is
  already made, and a cascade arrives to end the argument.
- **33 frees nobody.** Wei holds the line for **three hours and forty-one
  minutes** against a four-hour estimate — short of it, not over it — and the
  lattice is holding against a man who consented, which is the point the whole
  book has been building.
- **Milo is honest** about what he can hear, which is a building settling.

## Exit test

A reader can explain the entire solution without the thematic vocabulary: what
Harlow wanted, what Lyra refused, whom she asked, what could not be freed, what
remained unstable, what Wei did, why it worked, and what cannot be recovered.
