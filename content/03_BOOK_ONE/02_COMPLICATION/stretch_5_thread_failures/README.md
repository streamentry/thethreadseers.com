## What changed in this pass

- **The three-way crosscut is gone.** The draft cut between Lyra, Blackwood and
  Harlow in summary, and then listed the findings at the end as a bulleted
  summary of a bulleted scene. 15A is now one room, with people in it.
- **The causal claim is gone.** The draft had each discovery destabilising the
  Weave, which makes investigation itself the villain. Adeyemi now states the
  real rule in the room — *the number goes up when load goes up* — and the
  rising is attributed to the seventeen collectors being run at full.
- **The affected students are named before they are stakes.** The draft listed
  "Sarah Kim, Marcus Webb and Delia Roque", two of whom appear nowhere else in
  the book. 15A now uses the people the ending actually pays off: Maya Sandoval,
  Sam Whitfield, Maya Ruiz, and the exchange children. Maya Sandoval gets
  ninety remembered seconds so that chapter 23 does not introduce her cold.
- **Akira Harlow is cut.** The previous README here claimed he was "Mei's
  brother, eight years old, dead at Kyoto". He did not exist in the manuscript
  and canon removes him: Kyoto takes Aiko and Mei only, and there is no second
  dead child. The claim has been deleted rather than propagated.
- **Kenji's rescue has an engine, and it is not a vow.** It is a *records*
  problem: the site report says *no survivors detected* nine hours before the
  boy is found alive, Harlow can bury that with four lines from the Syndicate,
  and he refuses — and the refusal is written down as evidence against him on
  page four of his own file.
- **The fifteen/sixteen-year inconsistency is gone, and so is the wrong month.**
  Kyoto is **11 April** 1997 — the manuscript's own evidence was already April
  (a garden "in blossom", a festival, a recording dated 15 April) and the October
  appears to have been copied from the 1943 prologue. 1997 to 2013 is sixteen
  years everywhere.
- **The Council's prospective vote on the Nexus Stabilization Protocol is
  introduced here** and is the institutional clock the students cannot address.

## Exit test

A reader can say why the counter is at 45%, can say what Harlow wants in a
sentence that is not "power", and can say that he has refused one easy thing
and understands exactly what it cost.
