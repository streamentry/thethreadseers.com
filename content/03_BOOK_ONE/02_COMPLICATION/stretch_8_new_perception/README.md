## What changed

- **The ask is the chapter.** No private journey, no "is she captive?" panic
  about a girl who is not captive, no three days passing under a forty-hour
  deadline. The medical deadline and the global forecast are tracked separately.
- **Ch. 26B is new** and is what makes Ch. 28 possible and Ch. 30 achievable: it
  supplies the route, the second machine, the four names, and the fact that
  Harlow published a diagram with his own signature on it.
- **Milo's contribution is a person, not a power**: he counts a room by sound,
  carries the second case, and gets eleven-year-olds through doors.
- **Lyra never regains thread-sight.** She reads the machine with her hands, at
  close range, over four minutes, and that is how the reader learns what is in
  it.
- **The separation Ch. 30 needs is agreed on the stair** in 28, so that four
  people can be in two places without the book pretending otherwise.
- `The ensemble's thread-sight flickers and distorts` is gone.

## Exit test

A reader can say who is standing where, what the eleven minutes in the landing
decided, why the housing is a *hand* rather than a battery, and what the no
was.
