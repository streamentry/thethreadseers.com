> Full spoilers. Synchronized with the revised manuscript. This was the
> highest-priority structural repair area in the book and has been rebuilt.

**Question:** what does connection require when ability, trust and independence
have all changed at once?

**Exit after 25:** one settled rescue; defined states for two injured people; a
limited and *practised* non-visual method; a piece of procedure in force; a
changed but explicitly unfinished relationship with her father; and a man in a
locked ward who has told her about a second machine.

## What is now consistent

- **One rescue.** No second abduction, no machine reset, no competent arriving
  adult.
- **Wei Chen's medical trajectory** is a failing heart with an arrest and a
  stent, three to nine months, and a cardiology opinion. He is at the Academy
  from Ch. 12A onward and never "begins a journey to" it.
- **Milo cannot act with a gift he does not have**, at any point, in any chapter.
- **Lyra's ordinary eyesight is intact** and is written as ordinary. What she
  lost is visual thread perception.
- **Harlow's custody** is a chain: warrant, seizure order that fails, a
  supervised visit, a Council transfer, and a release by Blackwood in the
  confusion of Ch. 27. Nothing is conjured.
- **The counter is monotone** except for the fall in Ch. 20.

## Exit test

A reader can retell the rescue once, say what each injured friend can and cannot
do, name who is responsible for their care, say what the pact changes, and say
why the stand-down in 24B did not stop anything.
