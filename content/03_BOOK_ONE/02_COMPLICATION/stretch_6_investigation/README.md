## What changed in this pass

- **The revelation ladder is gone.** The draft ran `REVELATION ONE` through
  `REVELATION FOUR` as headings, and then summarised all four in a bulleted
  list. 17A is now one continuous forty-minute scene in which four people
  disagree about what to do with what they have found, and in which the fourth
  discovery is not a revelation but a *photograph*, which establishes presence
  and nothing else.
- **A photograph is used the way the book needs it to be used.** Lin is not on
  the Kyoto roster and she is in the garden. Eli is the one who notices that
  those two facts cannot both be true, and who says that a photograph cannot
  tell them which is the lie. This is the method Ch. 29 and the whole ending
  then need.
- **The missing escape is written.** The previous README claimed 17B ended with
  "Eli grounds the ward grid **by hand**". Nothing in the manuscript did this
  and nothing needed it to: the escape is a stairwell that rearranges itself,
  and the way out costs three injuries, the only copy of the original document,
  and eleven minutes of daylight.
- **The exam is gone from all three places it appeared** (15B, 18A, 19). The
  physical Maze has been rubble since Ch. 14, so an "upcoming Thread Maze
  practical exam" was impossible, and it also contradicted the forecast.
- **Ch. 19 now contains the report that goes nowhere**, which canon requires and
  the prose did not. The quartet write to the Council *through Security*, the
  copy to the Registrar arrives, the copy to the Council is held for eleven
  hours, and the minute authorising the live draw is forwarded to Harlow at
  11:40 with a marginal note in the Security Chief's hand. This is the second
  of the four concrete reasons in B09.2 for why the students have to act first.
- **Lin does not appear as a voice.** The draft of 19 had Lyra's mother *in the
  room*, whole and loving, telling her what to do. That is the alternative
  canon explicitly rejects. She is not there.
- **The team-up is not a team-up.** Four hands on a pile, on the grounds that it
  would change everything, was doing the work that Ch. 23's pact does properly.
  19 now ends on a notice sliding under a door.
- **Wei gets his second alert here**, in 18B, in Osei's hand, with the conflation
  refused in writing and the refusal to transfer him in writing, and Lyra carries
  it into 19 without telling anyone.

## Exit test

A reader can say what the letter proved, what the channel did to it, why the
warrant was a lie, what Lyra did wrong that was not "trying to save her
father", and what she took off Milo's.
