## What this file now contains

Only the coda. The **Nexus Concord** — which happens one week after the
climax — is no longer here. It is
[`03_RESOLUTION/stretch_10_convergence_preparation/34A.md`](../03_RESOLUTION/stretch_10_convergence_preparation/34A.md)
and is read *before* the six-week farewell, so that the book runs forward.

**Two years later**, from the November 2013 of the epigraph. **Corrected
2026-09-28:** this file was headed "Fifteen Years On" and said Lyra Chen was
thirty-two — both belonging to the **fifteen-year coda that was cut**
(`CANON.md` §7.4). The surviving coda is set in 2015 and 2016, and Lyra is
**eighteen**.

A door on the east corridor carries a note in Lyra's handwriting — twelve
initials and *the twenty-two other people whose names are on the board*, thirty-four
in all — and underneath it, in a nine-year-old's capitals: *I ASKED SOMEONE.*
(The board itself is Dr. Osei's and holds twenty-three names, `25:178`.)

**The coda's own internal dates, which the file did not previously state:**
the corridor on the second floor is **October 2015**; the letter arrives in the
**last week of November 2015**; Lyra is in the room from **March 2016**, and
"is there now". A header reading *twenty-one months later* pointed at August
2015, **before anything in the coda happens**; it now reads **Two years later**,
and the running system is "two years" old rather than the twenty months it
claimed.

## What the coda must not do

It must not retroactively soften the cost of Book One, must not guarantee
survival through sequels, and must not retire the four of them. It retires
nobody. The book it hands to Book Two has a standing threat, a sealed facility
in Lyon, and nineteen families who have been told.
*Corrected 2026-09-28:* this list previously included **"a form about a boy
called Ahmet"**. **There is no Ahmet in the manuscript** — a search across all
forty-six files returns zero, and the name appears nowhere in Book One. The open
file is now carried by **Eli Park's ninth item**, in his own margin, in pencil:
*"Ask somebody. I asked. It took four years… — E.P."* — and by his two-year
contract in a car park in Lyon. The hook is the letter he writes to the girl who
asks to be called something else, not a boy on a form. **Nothing has been
invented to replace it**; the false item has simply been struck.

**Ready when** a reader can say what ended, what was repaired over two years,
what is still open, and why a new student needs help — and can finish the last
page without being told what the theme was.
